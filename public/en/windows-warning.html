Does Windows show a warning when installing Legado?

Yes, and it's fine. Here is what to do.

What you'll see

1

When downloading, your browser may offer to discard the file: choose to keep it. In Chrome and Edge the option is behind the three-dot menu, next to the warning.

2

When you open it, a blue screen will appear: "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info".

3

After clicking "More info", the "Run anyway" button will appear. Click it to continue the installation.

Why it appears

Windows includes a security feature called SmartScreen. When you download a program, SmartScreen checks it against a reputation registry maintained by Microsoft: if the file is already known and used by many people, it installs without any notice; if it is new, or hasn't been seen on many computers yet, it shows that blue screen and asks you to confirm before continuing.

It is a reputation check, not a virus scan. SmartScreen is not saying it found anything harmful in the file: it is saying the file is not yet familiar to it. For the same reason, your browser may warn you that the Legado installer is not commonly downloaded.
